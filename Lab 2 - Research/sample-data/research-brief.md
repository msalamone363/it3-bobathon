# Research Brief — Sample

> Used in Lab 2, Exercise 2. This is a fictional research scenario for lab purposes only.

**Topic:** Selecting a vendor for enterprise document management

**Research Question:**
Which of the three shortlisted document management vendors best fits our needs for security,
ease of adoption, and integration with existing tools? What are the key trade-offs, and is
there any area where the available information is insufficient to make a confident decision?

**Background:**
The operations team is evaluating three vendors to replace the current shared-drive setup.
The main requirements are: strong access controls and audit logging (compliance requirement),
low friction for end users, and compatibility with our existing Microsoft 365 environment.
A recommendation is needed for the steering committee meeting.
