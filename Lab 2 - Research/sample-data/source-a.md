# Vendor A — Document Management Platform

> Used in Lab 2, Exercise 2. Fictional vendor profile for lab purposes only.

**Vendor:** Arcvault
**Deployment:** Cloud-hosted (SaaS), with optional on-premise agent for hybrid environments
**Pricing model:** Per-user, monthly subscription

---

## Key Features

- Granular role-based access controls with folder- and document-level permissions
- Full audit log of all document access, edits, and sharing events
- Automated retention policies with configurable expiry rules
- Native Microsoft 365 integration via plug-in (Word, Excel, Outlook sidebar)
- Mobile app available (iOS and Android)
- eSignature integration via third-party connector (additional cost)

## Security and Compliance

- SOC 2 Type II certified
- Data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- GDPR compliant; US data residency option available
- Multi-factor authentication supported; SSO via SAML 2.0

## Ease of Adoption

- Rated 4.1/5 on G2 for ease of use (based on 340 reviews)
- Average onboarding time reported as 2–3 weeks for teams of 50+
- Dedicated customer success manager included in Enterprise tier
- Self-service training portal with video walkthroughs

## Known Limitations

- Search functionality covers file names and metadata only; full-text search requires
  upgrade to Enterprise Plus tier
- Version history limited to 30 days on standard tier; unlimited on Enterprise
- No offline access; requires internet connection

## Pricing (indicative)

- Standard: $12/user/month (up to 100 users)
- Enterprise: $18/user/month (100+ users, includes SSO, audit logs, dedicated CSM)
